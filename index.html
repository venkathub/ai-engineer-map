The learning contract

Every topic ends in evidence.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Build a durable mental model from mechanism, constraints, and primary sources.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    Observe real tokens, traces, retrieval results, costs, and failure states.

  3. 03

    Modify

    Complete a focused implementation and make one trade-off visible.

  4. 04

    Build

    Deliver an independently tested artifact that belongs in a portfolio.
